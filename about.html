About Bhat Foundation Welfare Society

A registered social welfare organization based in Ompora Budgam, Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, working towards the upliftment and welfare of underprivileged and vulnerable communities.

Who We Are

Community-first, volunteer-driven.

Bhat Foundation Welfare Society is a registered social welfare organization based in Ompora Budgam, Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir. The organization works towards the upliftment and welfare of underprivileged and vulnerable communities through various social development initiatives and community support programmes.

The Society focuses on key areas such as education assistance, youth development, women empowerment, healthcare awareness, relief support, community mobilization, and livelihood promotion. Over the years, the organization has earned strong community trust through its grassroots outreach activities and volunteer-driven initiatives.

Bhat Foundation Welfare Society has extensive experience in coordinating with local institutions, community leaders, and stakeholders to implement welfare programmes effectively at the grassroots level.

With a dedicated core team and active volunteers, the Society continues to work towards social inclusion, humanitarian support, and overall community welfare in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir.

Our Vision

An inclusive, healthy, educated and empowered society.

To build an inclusive, healthy, educated and empowered society where every individual, especially the underprivileged and vulnerable communities, has access to equal opportunities, dignity, social support, and sustainable development for a better quality of life.

Our Mission

What we set out to achieve.

  • To promote social welfare and community development through sustainable initiatives and humanitarian support programmes.
  • To create awareness regarding healthcare, education, social issues, and community well-being.
  • To empower women, youth, and underprivileged communities through skill development, awareness programmes, and livelihood opportunities.
  • To organize free medical camps, educational support activities, and social welfare programmes for vulnerable populations.
  • To encourage community participation, inclusiveness, transparency, and sustainable development at the grassroots level.
  • To work towards building a healthier, educated, self-reliant, and socially responsible society.
