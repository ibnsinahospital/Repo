Our Projects

Bhat Foundation Welfare Society runs several ongoing projects in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir — from free medical camps to education support, women empowerment, drug awareness and community welfare programmes.

What We Run

Programmes & initiatives

Each project addresses a specific need in the communities we serve.

Medical Camps

Free multi-specialty and specialist medical camps offering consultations, diagnostic tests and specialist referrals to underserved communities.

View Camps →

Education & Youth Development

Live orientation programmes, career guidance and motivational learning for students, alongside support for education access in underserved areas.

Learn more →

Women Empowerment

Skill development, awareness programmes and livelihood-oriented support that help women move towards self-reliance and dignity.

Learn more →

Drug Awareness — Nasha Mukti Abhiyaan

Awareness camps, motivational talks and counseling support aimed at guiding youth towards healthy, drug-free lifestyles.

Learn more →

Community Welfare & Relief

Humanitarian assistance, relief support and welfare services for vulnerable individuals, families and communities in need.

Learn more →

Plantation & Environment

Community plantation drives and environmental awareness activities that promote a greener, healthier environment.
