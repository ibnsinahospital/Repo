Our Work & Programmes

Bhat Foundation Welfare Society works across several broad programme areas in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir — from healthcare awareness to education, youth development, women empowerment, drug awareness, community welfare and environmental initiatives.

What We Do

Our programme areas

Each programme addresses a specific need in the communities we serve. For specific completed projects, see our Projects page.

Healthcare Awareness

Free medical camps, health screenings and awareness on hygiene, nutrition, disease prevention and healthy lifestyles for underserved communities.

Education Support

Educational awareness, career guidance and orientation programmes that encourage students to stay focused on learning and build a better future.

Youth Development

Personality development, motivational learning and guidance that helps young people build confidence, direction and a positive approach to life.

Women Empowerment

Skill development, awareness programmes and livelihood opportunities that support women towards self-reliance, dignity and independence.

Drug Awareness — Nasha Mukti Abhiyaan

Awareness camps, motivational talks and counseling support aimed at guiding youth towards healthy, drug-free lifestyles and constructive activities.

Community Welfare & Relief

Humanitarian assistance, relief support activities and welfare services for vulnerable individuals, families and communities in times of need.

Plantation & Environment

Community plantation drives and environmental awareness activities that promote a greener, healthier and more sustainable environment.

Vocational Training

Practical skill-building programmes that support self-reliance, employability and livelihood generation for underprivileged youth and women.

Water & Sanitation Awareness

Awareness on safe water practices, hygiene and community sanitation to improve public health outcomes in underserved areas.

Our Approach

How we work on the ground

Every programme we run is designed around three core principles.

Community-First

Our work is carried out at the grassroots level with the participation of volunteers, professionals, educators, healthcare workers and community leaders.

Collaborative

We partner with local institutions, hospitals, schools and community organisations to maximize reach and effectiveness of every programme.

Transparent

We document our work through reports, photographs and updates so our supporters and the community can see the real impact of our efforts.
